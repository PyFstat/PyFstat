## Notes

- Pre-2025 PyFstat was all-human, with at most minor auto-complete usage in offline editors (VSCode and similar).
- Starting in 2025, GitHub CoPilot has been used on some changes, primarily for the test suite, GitHub actions, and other meta contents.
- All AI/CoPilot output was reviewed by a human.
- AI usage is likely to become more prevalent in future development, but full human review will always be maintained.
